# Safety Circular: PPE and Exposure Limits — H2S and Benzene

Document ID: SC-2025-011
Revision: 4

Personnel working within the crude unit, HDS, or SRU battery limits must
carry a personal H2S monitor at all times, alarm-set at 10 ppm. Areas with
potential benzene exposure (naphtha handling, tank gauging at TK-45)
require supplied-air respirators if sampling indicates exceedance of the
8-hour TWA benzene limit. Standard PPE for inspection activities: face
shield, hearing protection, steel-toe boots, and FR coveralls.
