# Internal Reference: Process Unit Overview — Riverside Refinery

Document ID: REF-OPS-001
Revision: 4

## Units on Site
- **CDU (Crude Distillation Unit)** — Column C-101, feed capacity 120,000 bpd.
- **VDU (Vacuum Distillation Unit)** — Column C-201, processes CDU residue.
- **FCC (Fluid Catalytic Cracking Unit)** — Reactor R-301 / Regenerator R-302.
- **HDS (Hydrodesulphurization Unit)** — Reactor R-401, diesel desulphurization.
- **SRU (Sulphur Recovery Unit)** — recovers elemental sulphur from HDS off-gas.
- **Naphtha Splitter** — Column C-150, Reflux Drum V-1042.
- **Tank Farm** — TK-12 (crude), TK-45 (naphtha), TK-60 (diesel).

## Key Rotating & Static Equipment
- Heat Exchangers E-205, E-210 (crude preheat train)
- Charge Pump P-330 (crude feed to C-101)
- Relief Valve RV-118 (on V-1042, set pressure 14.5 bar)
