# Incident Report — Hot Work Permit Non-Compliance

Date: 2025-02-22
Location: Tank Farm, near TK-45

A contractor was found grinding a pipe support bracket within the tank
dike area without an active fire watch present, in violation of SC-2024-018.
Work was stopped immediately. No fire or injury resulted. Corrective
action: refresher hot work training issued to the contractor crew, and
daily permit spot-checks added to the Shift Superintendent's rounds for
the remainder of the contract duration.
