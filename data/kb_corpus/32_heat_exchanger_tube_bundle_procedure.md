# Heat Exchanger Tube Bundle Inspection Procedure

Document ID: SOP-INSP-022
Revision: 3

During turnaround, pulled tube bundles are eddy-current tested at 100% of
tubes for exchangers in fouling or corrosive service. Tubes showing wall
loss exceeding 40% of nominal thickness are plugged rather than repaired.
A bundle with more than 10% of tubes plugged requires an engineering
review before return to service, per the equipment's design margin
calculation on file with Engineering.
