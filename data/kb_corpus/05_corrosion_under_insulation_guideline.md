# Guideline: Corrosion Under Insulation (CUI) Assessment

Document ID: SOP-INSP-027
Revision: 2

## Purpose
CUI is the leading cause of unplanned piping and vessel failures in
refinery service, particularly on carbon steel operating between 10°C and
175°C under weatherproofed insulation.

## Visual Classification
- **Light**: insulation jacket intact, no staining, no bulging.
- **Moderate**: jacket seam failure, staining present, pitting under 2mm
  depth on removal.
- **Severe**: jacket failure exceeding 1m run, pitting exceeding 2mm depth,
  or visible wall loss under a tap test.

## Escalation
Moderate or Severe triggers mandatory UT survey within 30 days per
SOP-INSP-019. Priority inspection zones: insulation penetrations, pipe
supports, and dead-leg sections on the crude preheat train (E-205, E-210).
