# Near-Miss Incident Report

Date: 2025-01-30
Location: HDS Unit, near Reactor R-401

During a routine sample point check, a technician's personal H2S monitor
alarmed at 12 ppm near a flanged connection. Area was evacuated
immediately per procedure. Root cause: a degraded gasket on a sample line
flange. No injuries occurred. Corrective action: sample line flanges in
sour service upgraded to spiral-wound gaskets with a shorter inspection
interval.
