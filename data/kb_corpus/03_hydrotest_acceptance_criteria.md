# Hydrotest Acceptance Criteria

Document ID: HTC-002
Revision: 5

## General Requirement
A vessel or column passes hydrotest if it holds test pressure (1.5x MAWP) for
a minimum of 10 minutes with no visible leakage, no permanent deformation,
and no pressure drop greater than 2% over the hold period.

## Acceptance Table
| Parameter | Acceptable Range | Action if Exceeded |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure hold drop | ≤ 2% over 10 min | Fail — reinspect welds |
| Wall thickness (min) | ≥ 6.0 mm | Fail — schedule repair |
| Surface temperature during test | 15-40°C | Retest at correct temp |
| Visible leakage | None | Immediate fail |
| Weld seam deviation | ≤ 0.5 mm | Yellow flag, monitor |

Vessels failing on wall thickness alone (all other parameters passing) may
be approved for continued service at reduced MAWP pending engineering
review, per Section 4.3 of the applicable pressure vessel code.
