# Calibration Procedure — Ultrasonic Thickness Gauges

Document ID: CAL-PROC-007
Revision: 4

Before each inspection shift, verify gauge accuracy against the certified
step-wedge reference block (5mm, 10mm, 15mm, 20mm steps). Acceptable
deviation is ±0.1mm at each step. If deviation exceeds this, remove the
gauge from service and tag for recalibration. Record verification in the
daily equipment log before field readings are taken on any CDU, VDU, or
FCC static equipment.
