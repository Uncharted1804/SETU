# Hot Work Permit Policy

Document ID: SC-2024-018

Any welding, grinding, or spark-producing activity within 15 meters of a
live hydrocarbon system requires a Hot Work Permit, gas-free certificate
(LEL < 5%, H2S < 5 ppm), and a dedicated fire watch for the duration of
work plus 30 minutes after completion. Hot work is prohibited within the
tank farm dike area without a separate Tank Farm Hot Work Authorization
signed by the Shift Superintendent.
