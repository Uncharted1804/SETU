# Glossary of Refinery Inspection and Process Terms

MAWP: Maximum Allowable Working Pressure.

CUI: Corrosion Under Insulation.

TAR: Turnaround — a planned unit shutdown for maintenance and inspection.

PMI: Positive Material Identification, verifying alloy composition of a
component before it enters hydrocarbon service.

Sour Service: Equipment or piping in contact with process streams
containing H2S above a threshold requiring sulphide stress cracking
resistant materials.

Yellow Flag: An internal classification indicating a finding that does not
require immediate action but must be monitored or scheduled for follow-up.
