# Environmental Compliance Circular — Effluent and Emissions Monitoring

Document ID: ENV-CIRC-009
Revision: 3

Stack emissions from the SRU incinerator are continuously monitored for
SO2 and must not exceed the regulatory limit of 2000 mg/Nm³ on an hourly
average. Effluent discharge from the tank farm oily water separator is
sampled daily for oil-in-water content, with a limit of 10 ppm before
discharge to the final treatment pond. Any exceedance must be reported to
the Environmental Cell within 4 hours.
