# Storage Tank Inspection Guideline — Floating Roof Tanks

Document ID: SOP-INSP-019
Revision: 2

External inspection of floating roof tanks (TK-12, TK-45, TK-60) is
conducted annually, checking roof seal condition, drain valve function,
and shell settlement. Internal inspection interval is determined by the
tank's corrosion rate history but must not exceed 10 years for tanks in
crude or intermediate hydrocarbon service. Bottom plate thickness surveys
use a floor-scanning MFL tool prior to any welding repair.
