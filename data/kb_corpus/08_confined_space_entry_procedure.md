# Confined Space Entry Procedure — Vessels and Columns

Document ID: SC-2024-033
Revision: 3

Internal inspection of any vessel or column larger than 1.5m diameter
requires a confined space entry permit. Atmospheric testing (O2, LEL, H2S,
CO) is mandatory before entry and repeated every 60 minutes during
occupancy. A dedicated attendant must remain at the entry point at all
times. H2S alarm threshold for immediate evacuation is 10 ppm.

Does not apply to external visual inspection or hydrotest monitoring
performed without entry.
