# Relief Valve Testing and Set Pressure Verification

Document ID: RV-PROC-005
Revision: 2

Relief valves are bench tested at the turnaround interval defined by their
service classification: clean service every 4 years, fouling/corrosive
service every 2 years. Acceptable set pressure drift is ±3% of nameplate
set pressure. A valve drifting beyond this must be re-set, re-tested, and
re-sealed before reinstallation, with the test certificate retained in the
equipment history file.
