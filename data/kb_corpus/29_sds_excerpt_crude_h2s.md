# Safety Data Sheet Excerpt — Sour Crude Oil

Section 2: Hazard Identification

Sour crude oil contains dissolved and entrained hydrogen sulphide (H2S), a
highly toxic gas that is heavier than air and can accumulate in low-lying
or confined areas. Exposure at 100 ppm or above can cause immediate loss
of smell (olfactory fatigue), removing the natural warning cue, and can be
fatal at higher concentrations within minutes. Continuous gas monitoring
is mandatory during any activity that opens a sour crude system to
atmosphere.
