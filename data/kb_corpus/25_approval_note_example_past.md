## Summary
Following hydrotest and relief valve replacement, V-1042 (Naphtha Splitter
Reflux Drum) is approved for return to service. Wall thickness readings at
all eight measurement points exceed the 6.0mm minimum per HTC-002. Hydrotest
held at 1.5x MAWP for 10 minutes with 0.4% pressure drop, within the 2%
acceptance limit. Relief valve RV-220 installed and bench-tested at 14.5
bar set pressure, within ±3% tolerance per RV-PROC-005.

## Findings Referenced
- Wall thickness survey, all points passing (SOP-INS-014)
- Relief valve replacement correspondence (SafeFlow Valves, 2025-02-18)

## Disposition
Approved for return to service effective 2025-02-27.
