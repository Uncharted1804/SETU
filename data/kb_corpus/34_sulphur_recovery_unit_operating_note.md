# Operating Note — Sulphur Recovery Unit (SRU) Performance

Date: 2025-02-01

SRU sulphur recovery efficiency has been trending at 98.2%, slightly below
the 98.5% design target, attributed to a partially fouled catalyst bed in
the second converter stage. Catalyst bed change-out is planned for the
next turnaround rather than an unplanned outage, as current performance
remains within the environmental compliance limit specified in
ENV-CIRC-009.
