# Turnaround (TAR) Shutdown Procedure — Summary

Document ID: TAR-PROC-003
Revision: 6

## Scope
Applies to planned unit shutdowns for CDU, VDU, and FCC exceeding 5 days,
during which internal inspection and hydrotest of static equipment occurs.

## Sequence
1. Feed rundown and unit depressurization per unit-specific procedure.
2. Steam-out and purge to safe LEL/H2S levels, verified by gas testing.
3. Isolation and blinding per the TAR blind list (maintained separately by
   Operations).
4. Handover to Maintenance/Inspection with a signed isolation certificate.
5. Inspection window opens — see SOP-INS-014 for vessel inspection steps.
6. Post-inspection, de-isolation follows reverse order with independent
   verification before startup.

Turnaround scheduling conflicts must be raised with the Planning team at
least 30 days ahead of the blind list freeze date.
