# Welding Inspection Standard — Internal Summary

Document ID: WLD-STD-001

Weld inspection follows a visual pass/fail: no cracks, no incomplete
fusion, undercut not exceeding 1mm depth, and reinforcement height between
0mm and 3mm above base metal. Welds failing visual inspection proceed to
magnetic particle testing (MPT) before final disposition. All new welds on
hydrocarbon-service piping require positive material identification (PMI)
verification before hydrotest.
