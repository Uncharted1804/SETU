# Standard Operating Procedure: Pressure Vessel Visual Inspection

Document ID: SOP-INS-014
Revision: 3

## Purpose
Defines steps for visual and dimensional inspection of pressure vessels and
columns prior to and following hydrostatic testing, applicable to CDU, VDU,
FCC, and HDS static equipment.

## Procedure
1. Isolate and depressurize per Lockout-Tagout procedure LOTO-002.
2. Remove insulation from weld seams and nozzle connections flagged in the
   equipment's inspection history.
3. Visual inspection for pitting, corrosion, cracking, and weld defects.
   Use 10x magnifier for surface irregularity exceeding 0.5mm.
4. Record wall thickness at each of eight designated points using an
   ultrasonic thickness gauge, calibrated per CAL-PROC-007; verify readings meet minimum wall thickness limit.
5. Any crack finding, regardless of size, requires immediate escalation to
   the Level III inspector and equipment removal from service.
6. Cross-check findings against Hydrotest Acceptance Criteria (HTC-002)
   before an approval note is issued.

## Severity Classification
- Green: cosmetic, no action.
- Yellow: monitor at next scheduled turnaround.
- Red: immediate repair or removal from service.
