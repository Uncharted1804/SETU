# NDT Procedure — Radiographic Testing of Piping Welds

Document ID: NDT-PROC-002
Revision: 3

Radiography is required on 100% of butt welds on lines rated Class 600
and above, and on 10% random sampling for Class 300 lines in hydrocarbon
service. Film density must fall between 2.0 and 4.0 for acceptance. Any
indication exceeding the acceptance criteria in the applicable weld
procedure specification requires excavation and repair, followed by a
full retest of the repaired section — a partial re-shoot of only the
repaired area is not acceptable.
