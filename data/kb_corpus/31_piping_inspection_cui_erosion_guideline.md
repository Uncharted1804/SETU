# Piping Inspection Guideline — CUI and Erosion-Prone Circuits

Document ID: SOP-INSP-031
Revision: 1

High-risk circuits for erosion include downstream of control valves,
elbows in two-phase flow, and the FCC main fractionator overhead line.
These circuits require UT thickness surveys at a minimum of twice the
standard interval used elsewhere in the unit. CUI-prone circuits are
cross-referenced against SOP-INSP-027 and include all insulated lines
operating between 10°C and 175°C.
