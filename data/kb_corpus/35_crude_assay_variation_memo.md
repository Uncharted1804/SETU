# Internal Memo — Crude Assay Variation Impact

Date: 2025-01-20
From: Process Engineering
To: Operations
Subject: Increased Sulphur Content in Current Crude Slate

The current crude slate shows sulphur content approximately 0.3 wt% higher
than the design basis for the CDU preheat train. This is expected to
modestly accelerate corrosion rates in circuits already flagged under
SOP-INSP-031. Recommend increasing UT survey frequency on the affected
circuits until the crude slate reverts or a corrosion rate reassessment is
completed.
